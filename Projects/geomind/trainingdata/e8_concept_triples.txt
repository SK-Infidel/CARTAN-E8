A star is a bright sphere in space that gives light and heat. The sun is the closest star to the earth. Light travels very fast through space and air.
Gravity pulls objects toward the ground. When you drop a ball, gravity pulls the ball down to the ground. When the ball hits the hard ground, the ball bounces back up.
Wood floats on water because wood is lighter than water. A small wooden boat can sail across a blue pond when the wind blows.
A sparrow is a small bird with feathers and wings. In autumn the wind grows cold and acorns fall from the oak trees. Animals gather food and seeds in autumn to prepare for winter.
A kitten is a young cat that needs warm milk and gentle care. When rain falls outside, a dry towel keeps a wet kitten warm.
Patience and careful work help us build strong things and solve hard problems. A clock uses gears and springs to measure time accurately. A key fits into a lock to open a closed door or chest.
